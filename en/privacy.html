Privacy & Terms

Mind & Biz is committed to handling your personal data with transparency and respect.

Last updated: June 2026.

1. Who we are

Mind & Biz is a business-focused marketing consultancy based in Brazil. For any privacy-related questions, contact us at mindandbiz@mindandbiz.com.br.

2. What data we collect

We only collect data you voluntarily provide by filling out the contact form:

  • Full name;
  • Corporate email address;
  • WhatsApp / phone number;
  • Company name (when provided);
  • Services of interest and budget range (when provided);
  • Message / project goals.

We do not collect sensitive data and do not engage in advertising tracking on this website.

3. How we use your data

Your data is used solely to respond to your enquiry, assess the fit between your needs and our services, and carry out the commercial conversation. We do not use your data for any other purpose without your consent.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. To operate the contact form, we use third-party processors: form submissions are handled by FormSubmit, and follow-up contact may occur via WhatsApp. These processors handle your data solely to facilitate the contact you requested.

5. Cookies and external resources

This website does not use tracking cookies. We load fonts (Google Fonts) and visual libraries from CDNs; this may result in the technical logging of your IP address by those providers in accordance with their own policies.

6. Your rights

You may at any time request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, write to mindandbiz@mindandbiz.com.br and we will respond promptly.

7. Retention and security

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described, or for the duration of the commercial relationship. We apply reasonable measures to protect your information against unauthorised access.
